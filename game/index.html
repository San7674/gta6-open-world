RUSTPORT

A rust-belt port city. One ex-dockworker. Five jobs. No way out but through.

Cole "Rook" Vane lost his dock job when the harbor dried up. Mara Kessler's Harbor crew offered him one shift. One shift became a life. Drive the city, steal what runs, dodge the Rustport PD, and finish the five jobs — or end up busted, broke, or worse.

W A S D — move / drive
Shift — sprint
E — enter / exit car (steal it if it isn't yours)
Space — handbrake  ·  F — punch
T — pick up a taxi fare
P / Esc — pause  ·  M — sound on/off

Original game. Rustport, its people and its streets are invented — nothing here is from any commercial game. Built for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty.
